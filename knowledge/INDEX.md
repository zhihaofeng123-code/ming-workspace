## About
Success is building **MING**, a consumer app that turns a person's birth date, time, and location into a Four Pillars (BaZi) chart and translates it into plain contemporary language across three areas: understanding yourself, understanding your timing (personal "seasons"), and understanding relationships (chart-to-chart comparisons, which double as the product's invite loop). Core habit is a two-minute daily ritual: one observation, one current theme, one action, one reflection question. Framing is conditions-and-choices, never prediction. (Stated by zhihao feng, 2026-09-01.)

## Business Goals
- Primary audience: English-speaking, astrology-curious people roughly 22-38 who already use Co-Star, The Pattern, CHANI, Human Design, Enneagram, attachment styles, or journaling apps, but know little or no BaZi.
- Secondary audience: Chinese diaspora and mixed-culture users who have heard of BaZi from family but never had it explained in a relevant way.
- Later audiences: couples, wellness/journaling users, therapists and coaches, people in career or life transitions, and broader Chinese-philosophy interest.
- Positioning: deeper and newer than Western astrology, without old-fortune-telling-website aesthetics.
- Pricing direction: launch well below CHANI's $107.99/yr category ceiling. Monetization and the sale model are deferred — zhihao feng wants a working demo first (stated 2026-09-01).
