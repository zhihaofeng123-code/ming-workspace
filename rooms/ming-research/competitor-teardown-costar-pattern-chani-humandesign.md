# Teardown: Co–Star, The Pattern, CHANI, Human Design apps

Prepared for MING (BaZi / Four Pillars consumer app). Compiled 2026-09-02.

**How to read this.** Every externally sourced claim carries its own source link on the line it supports. Store-listing prices and rating counts were read from the live listing during research on or about 2026-08-27 to 2026-09-02 and are marked "live listing"; they move, so re-check before quoting them externally. Anything I concluded rather than found is labelled **Inference**. Where sources conflict, both are shown. Where something could not be verified, it says so instead of guessing.

## 1. Co–Star

### Onboarding and time to first value

Co–Star's own FAQ states the sign-up sequence: "Download Co–Star for free in the iTunes or Google Play stores. / Enter your birth time, date, and location / Link your phone number or Apple account. / Enter the six-digit SMS code to verify your account… / Add friends to see your compatibility and read their daily updates." ([costarastrology.com/faq](https://www.costarastrology.com/faq)) Birth data is the only input — no quiz, no goal-setting: "We use your date, time, and place of birth to use astrology to generate the content that powers your astrology experience." ([costarastrology.com/faq](https://www.costarastrology.com/faq))

Notification opt-in and friend-adding sit *inside* onboarding, not after it: the flow "guides new users through profile creation, notification setup, and friend connection, personalizing their astrology experience." ([Mobbin flow index](https://mobbin.com/explore/flows/42bfbf5f-e870-4c74-94b2-efe823f6cfb3))

First value arrives immediately and overwhelms: "I entered the requisite birth time and location information, added a few folks, and was instantly overwhelmed by all the information. It breaks down your natal chart and those of your friends, and also how compatible you two are in seven categories… It was beautiful. It was a lot. I closed it, and didn't think about it again until March." ([Vanity Fair, 2019-05-22](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2019/05/co-star-astrology-app-notifications-founder))

A 2024 interaction-design critique names the same problem structurally: "the flow suffers from inconsistent mapping between steps… While users are asked to provide detailed birth information, the lack of clear mapping or visibility of the system's status leaves them unsure about what comes next," and "The app's choice of abstract icons and ambiguous phrases can confuse new users who may struggle to access key features like their natal chart or friend connections." ([ixd.prattsi.org, 2024-09-17](https://ixd.prattsi.org/2024/09/design-critique-co-star-ios-app/)) A separate UX case study reports from interviews that "many people download it, use it once, and never return" ([Medium, 2024-02-27](https://medium.com/@anicole.ramirez/when-being-pretty-is-not-enough-ux-case-study-app-co-star-df922a63225f)) — one designer's qualitative research, not a measured retention figure.

Exact step count and time-to-first-value in seconds: **not verified.**

### Pricing and where the paywall sits

Model, in the company's words: "Co–Star runs on a freemium model. 100% of our revenue comes from people paying for a la carte in-app purchases like manually adding friends and advanced chart readings." ([costarastrology.com/faq](https://www.costarastrology.com/faq))

US in-app purchases, USD, live listing (version 5.43, © 2026) — all ten rows from [apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561):

| Item | USD |
|---|---|
| Add Manually | $2.99 |
| Pro-Star monthly | $8.99 |
| Eros | $6.99 |
| 5 / 10 / 25 Questions | $2.99 / $4.99 / $6.99 |
| Crush Report – 5 | $4.99 |
| Your Year Ahead | $11.99 |
| Advanced Chart – Relationships | $8.99 |
| Advanced Chart – Self | $8.99 |

Prices are geo-tiered and actively tested. Canada: Add Manually $3.99, Pro-Star monthly $11.99, Your Year Ahead $14.99 ([apps.apple.com/ca](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561)). France: Add Manually 2,99 €, Pro-Star monthly 9,99 € ([apps.apple.com/fr](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561?l=en-GB)). India: Pro-Star monthly ₹999, Your Year Ahead ₹1,199 ([apps.apple.com/in](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561)). A Turkmenistan storefront snapshot lists Pro-Star annual simultaneously at USD 24.99 / 29.99 / 39.99 / 52.99 / 54.99 / 59.99 and monthly at 4.99 / 6.99 / 9.99 / 10.99 ([apps.apple.com/tm](https://apps.apple.com/tm/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561)) — **Inference:** that SKU spread is live subscription price-testing across cohorts, not a pricing error.

The $2.99 "Add Manually" price is unchanged since at least 2019: "Co—Star is free to download, but users can pay $2.99 to enter friends' or partners' birth information in the app." ([The Verge, 2019-10-04](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/4/20879631/co-star-astrology-app-zodiac-signs)) Subscriptions did not exist in 2021 — "The company plans to roll out subscriptions… later this year" ([Axios, 2021-04-14](https://www.axios.com/2021/04/14/astrology-app-co-star-raises-15-million-funding)) — so Pro-Star is a recent layer on an a-la-carte base.

**Paywall placement is the transferable part.** It is not at onboarding. Premium readings are "accessible at any time from your Settings tab. You can also find your own readings—or the readings of your friend—via links from both the Mars and Venus cards in your chart or in theirs." ([costarastrology.com/faq](https://www.costarastrology.com/faq)) The upsell is embedded *inside the free chart artifact*, at the card that already made you curious. The AI Q&A ("The Void") is metered rather than gated: "During their first visit, you can interact with the feature for free. To ask additional questions, you purchase credits… If you run out of credits, you can type a question, but you won't be able to submit it without buying credits." ([costarastrology.com/faq](https://www.costarastrology.com/faq)) You can compose the question; you just can't send it.

Clawing back free features has cost them goodwill: "a lot of the info that was free is no longer and there's barely any info that is available without paying for it… to take away what we already had access to has made this app unusable" ([Google Play review](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.costarastrology&hl=en)).

### Core retention mechanic

The daily push notification *is* the product, and an app open is explicitly not the goal. Founder Banu Guler: "I don't open my Co—Star notifications, and I feel good about that. It's just like, you get your message and you keep going with your day." ([The Verge, 2019-10-04](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/4/20879631/co-star-astrology-app-zodiac-signs))

The named daily unit is "Your day at a glance" ([Jezebel, 2019-04-02](https://www.jezebel.com/why-are-co-stars-daily-notifications-so-rude-1833747335)), a reading segmented into areas like "Work," "Thinking & Creativity," "Pressure," "Trouble," "Social Life" or "Sex & Love" ([Newsweek, 2019-08-03](https://www.newsweek.com/co-star-astrology-app-instagram-1451220)), plus daily do/don't lists — "there's a daily dos and donts list… lots of oddly specific things like 'do: extra…' and 'don't: ex's instagram'" ([appsupports.co review archive](https://appsupports.co/1264782561/co-star-personalized-astrology/positive-reviews)). Both store listings promise "Receive daily push notifications based on your horoscope for the day." ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.costarastrology))

The content engine is a hand-written corpus recombined against transits, which is why the cadence can be infinite: content is "snippets that are mapped to various planets and houses and signs and combinations thereof, and they get assembled by the AI, and remixed by the AI… The content team is writing a textbook, not a blog." ([The Verge, 2019-10-04](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/4/20879631/co-star-astrology-app-zodiac-signs)) Guler on the underlying selection: "it's based on a combination of your top transit and your placements" ([The Astrology Podcast Ep. 286, 2022-07-30](https://theastrologypodcast.com/transcripts/tap-ep-286-transcript-co-star-and-the-making-of-a-popular-astrology-app/)).

### Voice

Positioning line: "Co–Star is the astrology app that deciphers the mystery of human relations through NASA data and biting truth." ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561)) Homepage: "Astrology puts our temporary bodies in context with our universe's vastness, allowing irrationality to invade our techno-rationalist ways of living." ([costarastrology.com](https://www.costarastrology.com/))

Actual notification copy, verbatim: "Look in the mirror and ask yourself, 'Who's the boss?'" and "Pressure in Self: Stop pathologizing yourself." and "Get a plane ticket if you can and just go." ([Vanity Fair, 2019-05-22](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2019/05/co-star-astrology-app-notifications-founder)); "Your attention is a gift." / "You are not responsible for anyone else's behavior or shortcomings." / "Silence your ego." ([Vogue, 2019-09-06](https://www.vogue.com/article/whats-co-star-astrology-app-technology-spirituality)); "Today you are conflating strictness with rigor. Being skeptical won't help you. Use your good judgement to fail instead." ([HubPages, 2021-09-05](https://discover.hubpages.com/religion-philosophy/Astrological-App-Review-How-Real-is-Co-Star)); and the edge cases that generated backlash — "Your eternal rest nears. Goodbye." / "Start a cult." / "You are not meant for human consumption. Stay home today." ([Her Campus UCSC, 2021-05-18](https://www.hercampus.com/school/ucsc/why-co-star-deserves-no-stars-horoscope-horror-story/)).

The voice is a governed editorial system, not vibes. In a brand-book meeting, "slightly bored content writers decide that words like 'energy' and 'vibes' are *not* Co—Star, whereas 'ego' and 'attention' are much better," and the social lead described the voice as "a 'cool, older punk sister. You can smoke weed with us, but we'll read your letter to your crush.'" ([The Verge, 2019-10-04](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/4/20879631/co-star-astrology-app-zodiac-signs)) Guler on the deliberate contrast: "We live in this moment where all the startups can look the same, feel the same, kind of talk to people like they're toddlers… Everything's cartoon and curvy edges. But that's not real." ([The Verge, 2019-10-04](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/4/20879631/co-star-astrology-app-zodiac-signs))

They have softened, on the record: "We've gotten really much softer over time… especially starting once the quarantine started I think we all decided it was like not the time to be brutal and push," and "I don't think we tell people 'Start a cult,' anymore." ([The Astrology Podcast Ep. 286](https://theastrologypodcast.com/transcripts/tap-ep-286-transcript-co-star-and-the-making-of-a-popular-astrology-app/))

Epistemic framing worth stealing: "The question isn't whether astrology is real, the question is whether the effects are real... Is it a legitimate tool for self-care, and the answer is yes." ([Axios, 2021-04-14](https://www.axios.com/2021/04/14/astrology-app-co-star-raises-15-million-funding))

### Social and invite loop

The friend graph is the *first* bullet on both store listings, above the daily horoscope: "Add friends to compare natal charts and see your compatibility." ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.costarastrology))

Three distinct paths, and the structural choice matters:

1. **Free viral path** — add by username, phone contacts, Facebook friends (iOS), or "Quick Add… suggested friends based on your Co–Star network" ([Co–Star help centre](https://costarastrology.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13690142608013-How-can-I-add-friends)). Contact access is confirmed independently by the App Store privacy label, which lists "Contacts" under Data Linked to You ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561)).
2. **Paid non-user path** — $2.99 to chart someone who isn't on the app, sold as a premium feature: "See the full natal chart of somebody who doesn't have the app." ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561)) **Inference:** this converts curiosity about a non-user into revenue instead of into an invite — a deliberate trade of viral coefficient for ARPU, and the single most questionable choice in their loop.
3. **Screenshot path** — the notification is the shareable unit. The pushes "became a meme" ([Vanity Fair, 2019-05-22](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2019/05/co-star-astrology-app-notifications-founder)), the style "has spawned countless memes and continues to drive users to the app," and the app is "made to be shared on social media" ([The Verge, 2019-10-04](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/4/20879631/co-star-astrology-app-zodiac-signs)).

Comparison output is segmented into "seven categories, from 'Moods & Emotions' to 'Senses of Responsibility'" plus a composite chart ([Vanity Fair, 2019-05-22](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2019/05/co-star-astrology-app-notifications-founder); [Newsweek, 2019-08-03](https://www.newsweek.com/co-star-astrology-app-instagram-1451220)). Caveat on loop strength: "users have been less social and used the app more for self-reflection during the past year's pandemic, according to Guler." ([Axios, 2021-04-14](https://www.axios.com/2021/04/14/astrology-app-co-star-raises-15-million-funding))

### Scale and status

4.8 / 206K ratings on the US App Store ([live listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561)); 5M+ installs and ~96.1K reviews on Google Play ([live listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.costarastrology&hl=en)). Company-stated in 2021: "more than 20 million app downloads," and "downloaded by a quarter of all young women ages 18-25 in the U.S." ([Axios, 2021-04-14](https://www.axios.com/2021/04/14/astrology-app-co-star-raises-15-million-funding)). ~4.3 million monthly active users as of July 2026, attributed to Bloomberg ([TechCrunch, 2026-07-24](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/midjourney-acquired-the-astrology-app-co-star/)). Funding: $750K pre-seed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co%E2%80%93Star)), $5.2M seed April 2019 ([TechCrunch, 2019-04-17](https://techcrunch.com/2019/04/17/co-star-astrology-app-seed-round/)), $15M Series A April 2021 ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2021/04/14/astrology-app-co-star-raises-15-million-funding)).

**Material change:** Midjourney acquired Co–Star, closing spring 2026, terms undisclosed; Banu Guler became Midjourney's Chief Design Officer ([TechCrunch, 2026-07-24](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/midjourney-acquired-the-astrology-app-co-star/); [Gizmodo, 2026-07-24](https://gizmodo.com/were-at-a-crazy-moment-in-history-midjourney-buys-astrology-app-co-star-2000790434)). **Inference:** an image-generation lab owning the category's most screenshot-driven brand makes a visual/generative arms race in daily astrology content likely within 12 months. That is a bad place for MING to fight.

Circulating figures I reject: "over 30 million registered users" ([auraeastrology.com, 2026-03-29](https://www.auraeastrology.com/blog/co-star-app-review-2026-an-astrologers-honest-opinion)) is a competitor blog, unsourced, and inconsistent with the Bloomberg-sourced MAU figure; "$0 to $200 million ARR, no outside funding" ([gate.com, 2026-08-23](https://www.gate.com/news/detail/midjourney-makes-its-first-acquisition-by-purchasing-the-co-star-astrology-22971188)) contradicts ~$21M of documented venture funding and appears to conflate Midjourney's financials with Co–Star's.

## 2. The Pattern

The closest analogue to MING on both differentiators — it already owns a timing pillar and a chart-comparison loop. Read this section as the hardest competitive constraint in the file.

### Onboarding and time to first value

Inputs are name, exact date, time and place of birth ([Newsweek, 2019-07-12](https://www.newsweek.com/what-pattern-everything-you-need-know-about-app-channing-tatum-freaking-out-over-1449099)). The current web form asks Name / Birthdate / Time of birth / Place of birth with an explicit "I don't know my birth time" escape hatch, and warns "In some cases, birth time details may be slightly affected by daylight savings. If something does not feel quite right, try adjusting your birth time by one hour forward or back." ([thepattern.com/natalchart](https://www.thepattern.com/natalchart))

A third-party UX teardown counts "Onboarding steps: 7", paywall type "No Free Trial - Soft Paywall", and notes onboarding is fully gated behind account creation: "the onboarding process is entirely gated behind account creation (00:03), offering no guest mode to sample the experience." ([ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/the-pattern)) Its 119-screen capture of an 11:01 walkthrough timestamps account creation at 00:03, first content screens at 01:04–01:24, and "The Pattern paywall screen at 01:28" ([ScreensDesign capture](https://screensdesign.com/apps/the-pattern/)). So personalized content lands around a minute in and the first paywall lands seconds later. That is a vendor teardown, not a primary source.

First-hand: "The first thing I noticed when I downloaded The Pattern is that it gets straight down to business. After plugging in my birthday, time, and location, my pattern popped up based on my birth chart without any mystical colors or cute celestial animations to go with it" ([Bustle, 2024-05-15](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)).

What you see first is a personality read, not a chart wheel — "broken into three sections — Foundation, Development and Relationships — which are then divided into subsections for a total of six aspects of your personality, each with about 15 to 20 slides of text" (The Daily Dot, quoted in [Newsweek, 2019-07-12](https://www.newsweek.com/what-pattern-everything-you-need-know-about-app-channing-tatum-freaking-out-over-1449099)); daily content arrives as "bite-size pronouncements delivered in eight or so swipes" ([Vanity Fair, 2021-05-19](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2021/05/the-pattern-lisa-donovan)).

**The hidden-astrology framing — and its partial reversal.** For years the astrology was concealed: "its connection to astrology is not obvious or really advertised" ([Autostraddle, 2018-10-16](https://www.autostraddle.com/ruin-your-day-and-your-relationship-with-new-astrology-app-the-pattern-435133/)); "The Pattern avoids vocabulary about sun signs or aspects and instead opts for user-friendly personality passages that read like a Myers-Briggs result" (The Daily Dot via [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/what-pattern-everything-you-need-know-about-app-channing-tatum-freaking-out-over-1449099)); founder Lisa Donovan: "We don't consider ourselves an astrology app. This is a life app," and "We don't even do sun sign astrology." ([Bustle, 2022-01-10](https://www.bustle.com/rule-breakers/the-pattern-lisa-donovan-made-the-best-astrology-app))

That has been walked back at the wrapper level. The homepage now reads "The Pattern is a personalized astrology app" with a section titled "Your Transits" ([thepattern.com](https://www.thepattern.com/)); the Android title is literally "The Pattern: Astrology" ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thepattern.app&hl=en)); "Classes in Astrology 101, to master all things astro in understandable terms" is a paid benefit ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-pattern/id1071085727)); the natal-chart page openly argues Whole House vs Placidus ([thepattern.com/natalchart](https://www.thepattern.com/natalchart)); and a 2024 reviewer was told outright she was in "a Neptune transit" ([Bustle, 2024](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)). **Inference:** the durable lesson is not "hide the system" but "jargon-free in the insight, honest in the wrapper" — they discovered concealment costs you SEO, app-store search, and credibility, while jargon in the *reading* costs you comprehension. MING should copy the current state, not the 2018 one.

### Pricing and where the paywall sits

|---|---|
| Go Deeper+ Monthly | $14.99 |
| Go Deeper+ Quarterly | $29.99 |
| Go Deeper+ Annual | $83.99 |
| Connect+ Quarterly | $24.99 |
| 3 / 10 / 20 Custom Profile Slots | $9.99 / $16.99 / $19.99 |

Store description confirms the floor: subscriptions "may be purchased to unlock additional content starting at $14.99 USD per month" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-pattern/id1071085727); same text on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thepattern.app&hl=en)).

The paid tier roughly doubled in three years: $15/quarter in May 2021 ([Vanity Fair, 2021-05-19](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2021/05/the-pattern-lisa-donovan)) → £14.50/quarter or £36/year in the UK, July 2023 ([Hattie Crisell, 2023-07-07](http://www.hattiecrisell.com/new-blog/2023/7/7/meet-pattern-astrology-founder-lisa-donovan)) → $29.99/quarter by May 2024 ([Bustle, 2024-05-15](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)).

Free tier, per the company: "Daily personalized updates about you and your romantic partner or friends - Bonds that you can view to explore your compatibility with anyone - Access to some of our content library… Manage your Friends and Custom Friends" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-pattern/id1071085727)). Hard limits: "A new, free account provides you with three (3) daily Bonds to use each day… Your free bonds will be replenished the following day." ([The Pattern help centre](https://thepattern.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360045869212-How-many-Bonds-can-I-view-each-day)) Free time-travel is capped at ±1 year: "the Time Travel feature allows you free insight into what past and future transit cycles are in play - up to one year from the present date" ([help centre, 2024-09-12](https://thepattern.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409939517972-What-is-the-Time-Travel-feature)).

The paywall is contextual, at the point of depth: "Users get a free… summary of a topic… with a 'Go Deeper+' button that leads to the full, paid content… Paywalls are presented contextually whenever a user tries to access in-depth written content or listen to audio insights (01:27, 05:49)… There's no free trial offered." ([ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/the-pattern)) First-hand: "Sadly, though, the deeper explanations were behind a paywall, so I couldn't learn more" ([Bustle, 2024](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)). And from a notification: "I got a notification of my daily Pattern, and clicked 'Go Deeper' as always... to be surprised with an 'upgrade' pop-up" ([JustUseApp review archive](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1071085727/the-pattern/reviews)).

Price backlash is loud and specific: "$84.00 annually for premium?! have you guys lost your minds?" and "You even have to pay for bonds now!!" ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thepattern.app&hl=en)).

### Core retention mechanic: timing

This is the section MING's timing pillar has to beat, so it is worth reading in detail.

Verified in-app labels: "Your Transits" (current) and "Your Timing" (older), "Impacting Me Now", "Cosmic Climate" / "Cosmic Timing", "My Lens", "Time Travel", "Upcoming Transits", "Bond Transits", "Transit Ring". The string "Current Cycles" is **not verified** as a real feature name.

Product definition: "Your Transits: An informative overview of influential timing cycles you may encounter throughout your life - past, present and future." ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-pattern/id1071085727)) The original framing, still the best statement of the value proposition: "Your Timing offers an overview of what you're going through at this moment in time. These cycles are personal to only you. Understanding these dynamics can help you to navigate the difficult times and take advantage of the positive opportunities that you might otherwise miss." ([Newsweek, 2019-07-12](https://www.newsweek.com/what-pattern-everything-you-need-know-about-app-channing-tatum-freaking-out-over-1449099))

How it tells you what phase you're in:

- **Named cycles with start, end and peak dates.** "an overview of the dates and meanings of planetary cycles influencing you now and into the future" ([Bustle, 2024](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)). A reviewer describes the cycles as periods in which you might "make a connection of destiny", "change your approach to work" or "have a creative breakthrough", and notes "The app also shows the peak dates during which you might experience these cycles" ([Body+Soul, 2024-02-12](https://www.bodyandsoul.com.au/wellness/astrology/the-pattern-review/news-story/313fa03aee1c2d4b3356270a4ca0ed00)).
- **A weather metaphor, delivered as audio.** From the in-app transit explainer: "A transit, or a cycle, as it's sometimes called, is a temporary period of time where you're being impacted by a certain energy… Do you want to go out with an umbrella or a raincoat? Do you want to go for a fun walk in the rain? Or do you want to stay at home? In all scenarios, you know that it's raining and that you're being impacted by that, and it's going to change the course of your day. The same is true for a transit." ([Bustle, 2024](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)) This is conditions-and-choices framing, already shipped, already audio, already the top-of-funnel explainer. MING's "personal seasons" pitch is not novel against it.
- **An explicit "nothing much is happening" state.** The help centre has an article titled "What does Neutral Timing mean?" ([help centre index](https://thepattern.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/categories/360001999072-The-Pattern-Help-Center)) — the system models quiet periods rather than manufacturing drama. Article body not verified.
- **Time Travel.** "Located in My Vault inside your Impacting Me Now collection, the Time Travel feature allows you free insight into what past and future transit cycles are in play - up to one year from the present date… Subscribers… enjoy the experience of time traveling to any date in the past or future for themselves, romantic partners, friends, and custom friends!" ([help centre, 2024-09-12](https://thepattern.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409939517972-What-is-the-Time-Travel-feature))
- **Relationship timing as a separate paid layer.** Release notes: "Bonds Time Travel is here! Move through past and future dates to see how your relationship transits shift over time, with the same Transit Ring experience you already enjoy in Personal Transits." This is the most instructive category for MING, because Human Design is a *younger* and less historically grounded system than BaZi — "the newest system for self-discovery… brings together principles of the Chinese I Ching, the Hindu chakra system, Kabbalah, astrology, quantum physics and genetics" per its own onboarding ([HD Birth Chart onboarding capture](https://screensdesign.com/apps/human-design-hd-birth-chart/)); Vogue describes it as "created in 1987 by Ra Uru Hu" combining "astrology, the I Ching, Kabbalah, quantum physics, and Vedic philosophy" ([British Vogue, 2022-05-05](https://www.vogue.co.uk/arts-and-lifestyle/article/human-design-purpose)) — and it built a consumer category anyway. Provenance was not the growth driver.

### Social and invite loop

Second-person charts are the only real loop anyone in this category has built, and **nobody has a referral program.**

- Align's "My People" is a $0.99 one-time unlock to add other people's charts ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/align-the-human-design-app/id1577739358)), described as the growth engine: "The primary growth loop appears to be the 'My People' feature (04:50), which encourages users to share the app's insights with others, potentially driving word-of-mouth installs" ([showcase](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/align-the-human-design-app)). - HD Birth Chart treats partner *and child* charts as first-class flows, with dedicated chapters "Adding a Partner for Compatibility Analysis 02:57–03:40" and "Adding a Child for Relationship Analysis 03:49–04:46" ([capture](https://screensdesign.com/apps/human-design-hd-birth-chart/)).

**Inference:** every one of these monetizes adding a person rather than inviting one. Same structural choice as Co–Star's $2.99. Nobody has tested invite-to-unlock at scale in this category, which makes it an untested gap rather than a proven dead end.

## 6. Where MING can win

### 6.1 The timing pillar — win on structure, not on the existence of timing

The Pattern already ships personal timing, and ships it well: named cycles with peak dates ([Body+Soul, 2024-02-12](https://www.bodyandsoul.com.au/wellness/astrology/the-pattern-review/news-story/313fa03aee1c2d4b3356270a4ca0ed00)), an audio weather metaphor that is already conditions-and-choices rather than prediction ([Bustle, 2024](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)), a modelled "Neutral Timing" quiet state ([help centre index](https://thepattern.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/categories/360001999072-The-Pattern-Help-Center)), and free Time Travel out to ±1 year ([help centre](https://thepattern.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409939517972-What-is-the-Time-Travel-feature)). "We help you understand your timing" is not a differentiated claim against that.

What is differentiated is the **shape** of BaZi timing, and it maps onto three specific weaknesses in the incumbent:

1. **Nested, named, decade-scale structure.** Western transits are a bag of overlapping planetary periods with no canonical hierarchy; BaZi's Luck Pillars are a fixed, exhaustive, non-overlapping sequence of ten-year eras subdivided into annual and monthly layers. **Inference:** that gives MING something The Pattern structurally cannot render — a single legible answer to "which chapter of my life am I in, and when does it end," rather than "here are the four transits currently active." The Pattern's own copy sells the *benefit* of that ("navigate the difficult times and take advantage of the positive opportunities that you might otherwise miss", [Newsweek, 2019-07-12](https://www.newsweek.com/what-pattern-everything-you-need-know-about-app-channing-tatum-freaking-out-over-1449099)) while delivering a flat list.
2. **The freshness gap is open right now.** Users are actively complaining that the incumbent's timing content has gone stale: "over the last year or so they've gone full AI mode and all it's encrapified the app. The daily notifications are all the same and not informative" ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thepattern.app&hl=en)). Meanwhile CHANI, the highest-rated product in the set at 4.9/58K ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chani-your-astrology-guide/id1532791252)), cannot generate per-chart daily content at all because everything is hand-written to twelve rising signs ([Free & Premium Content](https://chaninicholas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500001732421-Free-Premium-Content-In-the-App)). **Inference:** there is a live gap between "generic and stale" and "human but only twelve variants," and per-chart daily generation grounded in a genuinely deterministic calendar is the way into it.
3. **The daily ritual is the right container and the field has left it half-built.** Co–Star proved a daily push can be the product with no app open expected ([The Verge, 2019-10-04](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/4/20879631/co-star-astrology-app-zodiac-signs)); The Pattern proved a morning check-in becomes habit ([Body+Soul, 2024-02-12](https://www.bodyandsoul.com.au/wellness/astrology/the-pattern-review/news-story/313fa03aee1c2d4b3356270a4ca0ed00)). Neither closes the loop with reflection. CHANI's journal is the only reflective surface in the set and it fires only twice a month, on New and Full Moons ([A Tour of the CHANI App](https://chaninicholas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/8711720295187-A-Tour-of-the-CHANI-App)). MING's four-part daily ritual — observation, theme, action, reflection question — would be the only design in this comparison that asks for a *return* input daily. **Inference, and weaker than the two points above — downgraded after review:** accumulated entries turn the daily unit from broadcast into a record, which is a switching cost none of these five has built, but no evidence in this file shows this audience will supply that input, and it asks more effort than Co–Star's no-open-required push ([The Verge, 2019-10-04](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/4/20879631/co-star-astrology-app-zodiac-signs)) — the only daily mechanic here with proven scale. Treat daily reflection as an unvalidated bet and design the ritual so the first three parts still land when the fourth is skipped.

**Also do these, they are cheap and evidenced.** Ship the "I don't know my birth time" escape hatch as an on-screen toggle with "You can change it later inside the app" ([capture](https://screensdesign.com/apps/human-design-hd-birth-chart/)), not buried in an FAQ — the hour pillar is MING's single biggest input-failure mode and it is worse than the Western equivalent, since without it the Day Master stem is intact but the hour pillar and its Ten Gods are simply absent. Be explicit about the accuracy cost the way CHANI is ([Unknown Birth Time](https://chaninicholas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411093003539-Unknown-Birth-Time)). And do not gate the chart behind account creation: HD Birth Chart reaches birth-data entry with no account ([capture](https://screensdesign.com/apps/human-design-hd-birth-chart/)) while Align, The Pattern and CHANI all wall it off, and CHANI's own teardown names that as its top conversion leak ([ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/chani-your-astrology-guide)).

### 6.2 The chart-comparison invite loop — the genuinely open gap

Two things are true at once, and both are load-bearing.

**The comparison feature itself is not novel.** The Pattern's Bonds are more developed than anything MING will ship in v1: no install required on the other side ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-pattern/id1071085727)), named tierable outputs built for screenshots ([Vanity Fair, 2021-05-19](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2021/05/the-pattern-lisa-donovan)), a public-figures database for cold start ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-pattern/id1071085727)), a 10-profile unlock quest ([help centre](https://thepattern.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360031597371-What-does-it-mean-when-I-share-a-Pattern-with-someone)), and a dating product built on top ([Daily Front Row, 2021-06-08](https://fashionweekdaily.com/soulmates-or-challenging-scarily-accurate-astrology-app-the-pattern-now-has-a-dating-feature/)).

**But nobody has built it as an acquisition loop.** See §5. Every incumbent charges for the second chart. **Inference:** MING's differentiated move is not "we compare charts" — it is making the second person's chart free *if they install*, and paid if they don't. That inverts Co–Star's $2.99 without giving up the revenue, and it is the one mechanic in this space with no incumbent precedent to copy from. Two caveats I cannot resolve from evidence: no source shows whether paid-add suppresses invites, and The Pattern's deliberate no-notification design ([help centre](https://thepattern.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360046293651-Are-people-notified-when-I-include-them-in-a-Bond)) may reflect a privacy constraint MING would also hit. Treat invite-to-unlock as the priority experiment, not a settled strategy.

**The elemental vocabulary is the differentiator; the asymmetry is a feature underneath it.** 合婚 is a real sub-discipline in BaZi, not a bolt-on: where Western synastry produces a similarity score, the Ten Gods produce a *directional* reading — who supports whom, who drains whom, which element one person supplies that the other lacks. Two separable things fall out of that, and after review I rank them the opposite way from my first draft. The **elemental language** — colour, texture, season, "she's the water to your wood" — is the part that survives one exposure and travels off-platform, and nobody in this set owns comparable vocabulary; The Pattern's screenshot-built outputs are compressible one-word labels ([Vanity Fair, 2021-05-19](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2021/05/the-pattern-lisa-donovan)). The **asymmetry** is a feature beneath it: it gives two people different things to react to instead of one shared percentage, and no incumbent in this set produces directional relationship output. Both rankings are **inference**, not measured.

**Social-risk caveat on asymmetry — it pulls against the invite loop above.** Directional output assigns roles, and "who drains whom" reads as a verdict as easily as a description. Nobody invites a friend to read that they take more than they give, so asymmetric output and invite-to-unlock reinforce each other only if the voice keeps the reading descriptive. **Inference:** ship asymmetry inside the invite path only when both directions are framed as conditions — what each person supplies and what each one needs — never as a score or a fault. No incumbent ships directional output at all, so this failure mode is untested by anyone.

### 6.3 Two structural advantages nobody in this set can copy

- **CHANI has no comparison feature at all and has left it open for five years** ([Gifting CHANI](https://chaninicholas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060346634-Gifting-CHANI); [Free & Premium Content](https://chaninicholas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500001732421-Free-Premium-Content-In-the-App)), while holding the highest satisfaction in the category at 4.9/58K ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chani-your-astrology-guide/id1532791252)). **Inference:** the category's willingness-to-pay ceiling is set by CHANI at $107.99/year ([App Pricing](https://chaninicholas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500001732281-App-Pricing)), and breadth of features is not what sets it. **Corrected after review:** neither is "depth and trust" in any form MING can buy — that price is backed by a decade of founder parasociality, a named person with her own press ([Vanity Fair, 2024-08-19](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/story/chani-nicholas-astrology); [Rolling Stone, 2018-06-01](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/meet-the-woman-bringing-social-justice-to-astrology-629153/)) and published receipts ([chani.com/about](https://www.chani.com/about/about-chani)). Treat $107.99 as a *destination* price the category has proven reachable, not a launch price MING has standing to charge.
- **Human Design proves provenance isn't the moat, but a named translator is.** A system invented in 1987 ([British Vogue, 2022-05-05](https://www.vogue.co.uk/arts-and-lifestyle/article/human-design-purpose)) built a consumer category on (a) a named human voice and (b) a ~75-second birth-data-to-identity-label loop ([capture](https://screensdesign.com/apps/align-the-human-design-app/)). Every AI layer in that category now claims authority through *whose corpus it read* — "It has studied all the source materials Jenna has ever created, and nothing else" ([myhumandesign.com/app](https://www.myhumandesign.com/app/)) versus "AI Ra is the only tool built on Ra Uru Hu's official teachings" ([tier FAQ](https://help.mybodygraph.com/en/articles/13340148-mybodygraph-tier-faq)). **Inference:** MING's thousand years of lineage is worth nothing as a fact and a great deal as a *corpus claim* — but only if there is a named, attributable teaching source behind the interpretations. Anonymous authority is the weakest position available here.

## 7. Where competing head-on is a mistake

**Do not fight Co–Star on the brutal-witty daily notification.** It is a governed editorial system with a documented brand book that rules words in and out ([The Verge, 2019-10-04](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/4/20879631/co-star-astrology-app-zodiac-signs)), a decade of corpus, 4.8/206K ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561)), ~4.3M MAU ([TechCrunch, 2026-07-24](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/midjourney-acquired-the-astrology-app-co-star/)), meme-level cultural saturation ([Vanity Fair, 2019-05-22](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2019/05/co-star-astrology-app-notifications-founder)) — and it is now owned by an image-generation lab ([Gizmodo, 2026-07-24](https://gizmodo.com/were-at-a-crazy-moment-in-history-midjourney-buys-astrology-app-co-star-2000790434)). **Inference:** a visual and generative escalation in shareable daily astrology content is the likeliest near-term consequence of that acquisition. Competing on wit-per-notification is competing on their strongest axis at the exact moment they get more resources for it. MING's daily unit should win on *usefulness and continuity*, not on being funnier.

**Do not fight CHANI on human-authored authority or on values positioning — and do not imagine you can abstain from that fight either.** Their whole identity is "made by real astrologers — not AI" and "a queer, feminist-led company that is anti-AI and not VC-funded" ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.chani_nicholas_inc.chani&hl=en_US)), backed by a real founder brand, real journalism ([Vanity Fair, 2024-08-19](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/story/chani-nicholas-astrology); [Rolling Stone, 2018-06-01](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/meet-the-woman-bringing-social-justice-to-astrology-629153/)), and receipts — 5% of revenue donated, "over $3.5 million for survivors to date," an $80k salary floor ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chani-your-astrology-guide/id1532791252); [chani.com/about](https://www.chani.com/about/about-chani)). An AI-assisted app arriving with a values claim it cannot yet evidence will read as hollow next to that. **Revised after review:** the line is already drawn — "not AI" is CHANI's own framing on its store listing ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.chani_nicholas_inc.chani&hl=en_US)) and §6.1's recommendation, per-chart daily generation, puts MING on the wrong side of it by default. Answering with "craft and rigour" is a deletion rule, not a position: it tells a writer what to cut, every competitor would sign it, and it leaves the authority anonymous — the weakest place to stand, per 6.3.

**The counter-axis is derivation, not virtue. Inference:** BaZi runs on a fixed calendar — the same birth data yields the same four pillars for anyone who computes them, and every interpretation line can be traced to a named element in the chart. Showing that derivation under each line is a claim the reader can check, and CHANI structurally cannot match it, because its daily and weekly content is hand-written to twelve rising signs rather than derived per chart ([Free & Premium Content](https://chaninicholas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500001732421-Free-Premium-Content-In-the-App)). **One limit on that claim:** computability alone differentiates nothing — Western natal charts are ephemeris-derived too — so the position is not "our system is deterministic" but "this line came from your hour pillar, and here it is." That is a stance with a speaker and a falsifiable promise; the risk is that it also makes every weak interpretation auditable, which is a product-quality bar before it is a brand line. No source here tests whether this audience values shown work, so it stays an inference.

**Do not fight The Pattern on the relationship-comparison feature *set*.** Bonds, custom profiles, public figures, most-like/least-like, Bond Transits, Romantic Timing, Super Connect and a dating product are years of accumulated surface ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-pattern/id1071085727); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thepattern.app&hl=en); [Vanity Fair, 2021-05-19](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2021/05/the-pattern-lisa-donovan)). A v1 that tries to match feature-for-feature loses on completeness. Compete on the *shape* of the output (asymmetric, elemental, directional) and on the *economics* of the loop (invite-to-unlock), not on the feature list.

**Do not build a dating product.** The Pattern got there in 2021 with a geographic rollout and a paid signalling mechanic ([Daily Front Row, 2021-06-08](https://fashionweekdaily.com/soulmates-or-challenging-scarily-accurate-astrology-app-the-pattern-now-has-a-dating-feature/); [Vanity Fair, 2021-05-19](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2021/05/the-pattern-lisa-donovan)), and it requires liquidity MING will not have. The comparison loop should point at people the user already knows.

**Do not become the deep, accurate, expert-facing BaZi tool.** That is exactly what Human Design App (Healing Shore) is — 4.8★ from a decade of loyal users ([mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/human-design-app/862678884)), leading its store copy with "Natal, Composite, Group and Return Charts… Color, Tone, Base information" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/human-design-app/id862678884)) — and it sits at 10K+ Google Play downloads ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.HumanDesign.Google&hl=en)) while the jargon-free brand play reaches 100K+ ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.myhumandesignapp)). The same trap in expensive form is myBodyGraph at $349–$1,199/yr ([pricing](https://www.mybodygraph.com/pricing)) discounting 40% to hold the line ([current offers](https://help.mybodygraph.com/en/articles/16259171-current-offers)). Ten Gods, hidden stems and clashes belong in the reference layer, never in acquisition copy.

**Do not price weekly, and do not claw back free features.** Weekly pricing up to $8.99/wk with the paywall placed before any result ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/human-design-hd-birth-chart/id1582464555); [showcase](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/human-design-hd-birth-chart)) is a paid-acquisition arbitrage that buys no word of mouth. And every product here that moved free content behind the wall took visible, durable reputational damage: Co–Star ("to take away what we already had access to has made this app unusable" — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.costarastrology&hl=en)), The Pattern ("You even have to pay for bonds now!!" — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thepattern.app&hl=en)), CHANI ("nearly everything is behind a pay wall, including information from your CHART" — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.chani_nicholas_inc.chani&hl=en_US)), Align ("Now you're telling me to see the actual layout of my chart, I have to have a subscription" — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.myhumandesignapp)). **Inference:** set the free line at chart-plus-daily-ritual on day one and never move it inward; put the paywall inside the free artifact the way Co–Star does with its Mars and Venus cards ([FAQ](https://www.costarastrology.com/faq)).

## 8. Open questions this teardown could not answer

- No DAU, retention curve, churn or trial-conversion figure is public for any of the five products. Every retention claim here is inferred from feature presence and user testimony, not measured.
- Whether paid second-chart adds suppress invites relative to invite-to-unlock: untested by anyone in the category, so far as the sources show.
- Exact onboarding step counts and time-to-first-value for Co–Star: not verified.
- Third-party revenue and install estimates conflict badly across and within vendors and were not used for any conclusion above.
- Company-supplied user counts (The Pattern's 7.5M/15M/18M, Align's "seven million") are unaudited and use non-reconcilable units.
